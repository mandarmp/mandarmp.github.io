One-paragraph thesis: what is actually hard in BCI right now (signal quality,
longevity, decoding, regulation, reimbursement) and where the progress is.

## The approaches
- Invasive (intracortical arrays, ECoG): bandwidth vs. surgical risk.
- Minimally invasive (endovascular, thin-film): the middle ground.
- Non-invasive (EEG, fNIRS, ultrasound): scale vs. signal.

## The real bottlenecks
- Signal quality and electrode longevity.
- Decoders that generalise across days and users.
- Latency budgets for closed-loop control.
- Regulatory path and clinical endpoints.

## How I would evaluate a BCI company's data claims
- Offline accuracy vs. online, closed-loop performance.
- Cross-session and cross-subject validation (leakage!).
- Channel count vs. usable channels.
- Who the first paying customer is.

## Takeaways
Three bullet points an investor or partner could act on.
