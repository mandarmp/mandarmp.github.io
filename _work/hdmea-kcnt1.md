## The problem

*KCNT1* encodes a sodium-activated potassium channel that regulates neuronal
excitability. Pathogenic variants cause severe childhood epilepsies,
including developmental and epileptic encephalopathy. Outcomes vary widely and
there are no targeted treatments. To test new drugs you first need a reliable,
quantitative readout of how mutant neuronal networks behave differently.

## What we built

High-density microelectrode array (HD-MEA) chips record thousands of
electrodes at once from cultured neurons and acute slices of mouse frontal
cortex, preserving native circuit architecture. The analysis pipeline:

- **Spike sorting:** SpikeInterface, Kilosort and Phy, plus the lab's own
  methods, to turn raw voltage into single-unit activity.
- **Network metrics:** firing rates, burst detection and synchrony to
  characterise network dynamics.
- **Comparison:** wild-type vs. *KCNT1* gain-of-function networks, and a
  baseline for testing pharmacological treatments.
