## Overview

CORLEGO is a computational model of visual attention. My contribution focused
on the modelling components and on turning the research methods into workflows
that others can re-run and extend.

## Highlights

- Implemented model components for attention analysis.
- Packaged the analysis so results can be reproduced from raw inputs.
- Published as a peer-reviewed article in **PeerJ**.
