CH05 About

Engineer by training, neuroscientist by curiosity.

I design end-to-end computational workflows that turn noisy, high-volume biosignal data into reliable, decision-ready outputs.

I started my career as a software engineer at Bosch, modernising C++ codebases and owning CI/CD releases. I then studied computational neuroscience and cognitive robotics at the University of Birmingham, and at UC Davis I've built HD-MEA and EEG analysis platforms for research on autism and epilepsy. I'm now a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering.

I bring scientific rigour and production habits to the same work: robust code, reproducible pipelines and performance you can measure.
