PhD Student · Biomedical Engineering · UC Davis

Mandar Patil

I build data and machine-learning pipelines for high-dimensional neural recordings, turning noisy electrophysiology into reliable, reproducible results.

My work spans high-density microelectrode arrays (HD-MEA), EEG and computational models of neurodevelopmental disorders such as autism and epilepsy. Before research I was a software engineer at Bosch, and I hold an MSc in Computational Neuroscience & Cognitive Robotics from the University of Birmingham.
