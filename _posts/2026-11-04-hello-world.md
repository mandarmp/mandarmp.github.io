For years most of my work has lived in lab servers, private repositories and
slide decks. This blog is where I'll write some of it up properly.

## What to expect

- **Neural data engineering:** lessons from processing high-density
  microelectrode array (HD-MEA) and EEG recordings at scale.
- **ML in practice:** validation pitfalls, feature engineering for signals,
  and getting models out of notebooks.
- **Research software:** reproducible pipelines, HPC workflows and the
  CI/CD habits I picked up as a software engineer at Bosch.
- **Notes from the PhD:** papers I'm reading and things I'm learning.

## Why bother?

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain a pipeline step simply, I probably
don't understand it well enough. Writing in public also makes the work easier to
share with collaborators, students and future teammates.
